Judge calibration labelling

Read the job, then the proposal, and give the score you think it deserves. Don't overthink it: about 30–60 s per item. Keys: 1–5 to score, ←/→ to move.

Rubric: what each score means
  • 5 Excellent: answers this client's actual need; evidence is relevant to their requirements with concrete results; clear plan and next step. You'd hire this person.
  • 4 Good: relevant and backed by evidence, but one weakness (one requirement not covered, a slightly generic part, or a vague plan).
  • 3 OK / generic: on topic, but could be sent to many similar jobs; evidence is thin or only partly relevant.
  • 2 Weak: mostly generic or mismatched evidence (projects that don't relate to what the client asked); key requirements ignored.
  • 1 Bad: irrelevant, unbelievable, broken or empty text, or wrong job entirely.

Quick checks: (1) Does it address what this client asked for? (2) Is the evidence related to those requirements? (3) Are there specific results? (4) Is there a clear plan / next step? (5) Does it read naturally?
