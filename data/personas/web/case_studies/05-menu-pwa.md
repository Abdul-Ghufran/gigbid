# Restaurant ordering PWA

## The problem
Waiters were overwhelmed during rush hour and orders got lost.

## What I did
- I designed the piece that handles reactjs so the system scales cleanly.
- The solution relied on utility-first styling; I wrote that part myself to reduce operating cost.
- I developed the piece that handles Node.js to reduce operating cost.
- I implemented the piece that handles mongo so non-technical staff could use it.

## Outcome
The work moved 46% of orders to the app in 3 months.

_Timeline: about 3 weeks. Client industry: hospitality._
