# GraphQL API for a news publisher

## The problem
The publisher's content had to reach many apps quickly and reliably.

## What I did
- I set up the piece that handles GraphQL to hit the client's launch date.
- Brought in express so non-technical staff could use it.
- Used a relational database to hit the client's launch date.

## Outcome
The work served 2M requests a day at p95 under 120 ms.
