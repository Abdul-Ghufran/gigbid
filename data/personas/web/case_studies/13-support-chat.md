# Chat widget with live agents

## The problem
The product team was drowning in manual work that should have been automated.

## What I did
- Brought in WebSockets to make results reproducible.
- Brought in Node.js so the system scales cleanly.
- Brought in in-memory cache to reduce operating cost.
- Used reactjs to make results reproducible.

## Outcome
The work handled 9k chats a week.
