# Learning platform with video courses

## The problem
Admissions staff answered the same questions hundreds of times a week.

## What I did
- I shipped the piece that handles nextjs to hit the client's launch date.
- I built the core of it with took card payments and subscriptions, to make results reproducible.
- The solution relied on mongoose; I delivered that part myself so the system scales cleanly.

## Outcome
The work onboarded 5,400 students.

_Timeline: about 8 weeks. Client industry: education._
