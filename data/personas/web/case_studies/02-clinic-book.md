# Clinic booking system with reminders

## The problem
Clinical staff lost hours every shift to scheduling and paperwork.

## What I did
- I developed the core of it with batteries-included Python web framework, to keep latency low under load.
- Used psql so the system scales cleanly.
- I wrote the core of it with task queue, to hit the client's launch date.

## Outcome
The work cut no-shows by 31% with SMS reminders.

_Timeline: about 5 weeks. Client industry: healthcare._
