# Event ticketing with QR check-in

## The problem
Ticket fraud and slow gates were ruining the first hour of every show.

## What I did
- Used Django so non-technical staff could use it.
- Brought in Stripe so non-technical staff could use it.
- Brought in component-based front end to hit the client's launch date.

## Outcome
The work sold 38k tickets with zero double-scans.

_Timeline: about 12 weeks. Client industry: events._
