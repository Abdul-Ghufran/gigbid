# SaaS billing portal rebuild

## The problem
The product team was drowning in manual work that should have been automated.

## What I did
- The solution relied on reactjs; I designed that part myself so the team could stop doing it by hand.
- Brought in TypeScript to reduce operating cost.
- Used took card payments and subscriptions so non-technical staff could use it.
- I wrote the core of it with express, to make results reproducible.

## Outcome
The work reduced failed renewals by 18%.

_Timeline: about 5 weeks. Client industry: saas._
