# Accessibility overhaul of a government portal

## The problem
Citizens using assistive technology could not complete basic forms.

## What I did
- The solution relied on React; I built that part myself so the team could stop doing it by hand.
- I built the piece that handles ts to reduce operating cost.
- Used Playwright to keep latency low under load.

## Outcome
The work went from 41 to 0 WCAG AA violations.

_Timeline: about 14 weeks. Client industry: government._
