# Real-time logistics tracking dashboard

## The problem
Dispatchers had no live view of where vehicles were.

## What I did
- The solution relied on component-based front end; I implemented that part myself so the team could stop doing it by hand.
- Brought in WebSockets so the team could stop doing it by hand.
- The solution relied on Node.js; I implemented that part myself to keep latency low under load.

## Outcome
The work tracked 600 vehicles with sub-second updates.
