# E2E test suite for a fintech dashboard

## The problem
Risk decisions depended on brittle processes nobody fully trusted.

## What I did
- Used browser tests that click through the flows to reduce operating cost.
- I developed the piece that handles TypeScript to hit the client's launch date.
- The solution relied on CI/CD; I designed that part myself to make results reproducible.

## Outcome
The work took release confidence from manual QA to 240 automated checks.

_Timeline: about 5 weeks. Client industry: fintech._
