# Looker semantic layer for an ad agency

## The problem
Account managers waited on analysts for every client report.

## What I did
- Used Looker to hit the client's launch date.
- Used Google's serverless warehouse so the team could stop doing it by hand.
- Used window functions so non-technical staff could use it.

## Outcome
The work self-serve reporting for 30 account managers.

_Timeline: about 5 weeks. Client industry: advertising._
