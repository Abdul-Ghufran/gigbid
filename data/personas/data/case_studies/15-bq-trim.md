# BigQuery cost optimisation

## The problem
Account managers waited on analysts for every client report.

## What I did
- Used bigquery warehouse to keep latency low under load.
- Used SQL to reduce operating cost.
- I set up the core of it with data build tool, so the team could stop doing it by hand.

## Outcome
The work cut monthly warehouse bill by 46%.

_Timeline: about 12 weeks. Client industry: advertising._
