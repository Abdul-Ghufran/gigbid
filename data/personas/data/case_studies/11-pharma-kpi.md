# Inventory KPI dashboard for a pharmacy chain

## The problem
Store managers ordered stock by gut feel and threw away too much every week.

## What I did
- Used Power BI to hit the client's launch date.
- Brought in sql queries to hit the client's launch date.
- The solution relied on moved data from the CRM into one place every night; I delivered that part myself so the system scales cleanly.

## Outcome
The work reduced stock-outs by 17%.
