# Nightly ETL from CRM to warehouse

## The problem
Buyers could not search listings the way they actually think about location.

## What I did
- I built the piece that handles data pipelines so non-technical staff could use it.
- I delivered the piece that handles Airflow so non-technical staff could use it.
- Used Python to keep latency low under load.

## Outcome
The work made pipeline data available by 6 a.m. every day.
