# Sales performance dashboard for a distributor

## The problem
Sales reports were assembled by hand from five exports every month.

## What I did
- Used Power BI to make results reproducible.
- I delivered the core of it with wrote the queries behind every number, to make results reproducible.
- Used Excel so non-technical staff could use it.

## Outcome
The work cut the monthly reporting cycle from 5 days to 1.

_Timeline: about 4 weeks. Client industry: wholesale._
