# Demand forecast in spreadsheets for a bakery

## The problem
The bakery baked the same amount every day regardless of demand.

## What I did
- The solution relied on pivot tables; I set up that part myself so the team could stop doing it by hand.
- The solution relied on predicted next month's demand; I built that part myself so the team could stop doing it by hand.

## Outcome
The work cut daily waste by 14%.
