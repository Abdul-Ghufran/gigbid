# dbt models for a SaaS warehouse

## The problem
The product team was drowning in manual work that should have been automated.

## What I did
- The solution relied on dbt; I built that part myself so the system scales cleanly.
- The solution relied on snowflake warehouse; I implemented that part myself so non-technical staff could use it.
- Brought in SQL to make results reproducible.

## Outcome
The work replaced 60 ad-hoc queries with 24 tested models.
