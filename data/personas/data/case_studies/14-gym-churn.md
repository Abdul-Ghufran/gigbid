# Churn drivers analysis for a gym chain

## The problem
Members were cancelling and management did not know why.

## What I did
- I implemented the core of it with wrote the queries behind every number, to hit the client's launch date.
- Used Statistics so non-technical staff could use it.
- Brought in Tableau to keep latency low under load.

## Outcome
The work linked 40% of churn to class-schedule changes.

_Timeline: about 9 weeks. Client industry: fitness._
