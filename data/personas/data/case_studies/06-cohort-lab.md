# Customer cohort and retention analysis

## The problem
Many new customers ordered once and never came back.

## What I did
- I implemented the piece that handles sql queries so non-technical staff could use it.
- Brought in dataframes to hit the client's launch date.
- The solution relied on charts; I wrote that part myself to make results reproducible.

## Outcome
The work identified the week-2 drop-off that drove a new onboarding flow.
