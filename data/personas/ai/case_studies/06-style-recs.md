# Product recommendations for a fashion store

## The problem
The store had traffic but too few visitors turned into buyers.

## What I did
- Used suggested what each shopper should see next to make results reproducible.
- I implemented the core of it with python3, to keep latency low under load.

## Outcome
The work raised add-to-cart rate by 11% in an A/B test.

_Timeline: about 8 weeks. Client industry: ecommerce._
