# Multi-agent research assistant

## The problem
Analysts spent two days on every first-draft market brief.

## What I did
- I built the core of it with LangChain, to reduce operating cost.
- The solution relied on document Q&A bot; I built that part myself so the team could stop doing it by hand.
- Used Python to reduce operating cost.

## Outcome
The work produced first-draft market briefs in 6 minutes instead of 2 days.

_Timeline: about 5 weeks. Client industry: consulting._
