# Credit-risk scorecard refresh

## The problem
Risk decisions depended on brittle processes nobody fully trusted.

## What I did
- Brought in scikit-learn so the team could stop doing it by hand.
- Brought in Statistics to make results reproducible.
- The solution relied on pandas; I wrote that part myself so the system scales cleanly.

## Outcome
The work improved Gini from 0.41 to 0.52 on the holdout.
