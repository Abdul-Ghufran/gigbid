# Demand forecasting for a grocery chain

## The problem
Store managers ordered stock by gut feel and threw away too much every week.

## What I did
- Brought in predicted next month's demand to make results reproducible.
- I built the piece that handles dataframes so non-technical staff could use it.
- I delivered the core of it with Python, to hit the client's launch date.

## Outcome
The work lowered MAPE from 24% to 13% and cut waste by 9%.

_Timeline: about 3 weeks. Client industry: retail._
