# Invoice field extraction with OCR

## The problem
Staff keyed invoice fields into the ledger by hand.

## What I did
- Used Computer vision to keep latency low under load.
- Used made sense of free-text tickets so non-technical staff could use it.
- Brought in Python to keep latency low under load.

## Outcome
The work extracted 12 fields at 96% accuracy across 30 vendor layouts.
