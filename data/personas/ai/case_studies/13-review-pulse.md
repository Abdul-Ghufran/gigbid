# Sentiment dashboard for app reviews

## The problem
Thousands of app-store reviews arrived daily and nobody read them.

## What I did
- The solution relied on NLP; I implemented that part myself to make results reproducible.
- I developed the core of it with Data visualization, so non-technical staff could use it.
- Used Python so the team could stop doing it by hand.

## Outcome
The work surfaced 3 crash-related themes a week before support noticed.

_Timeline: about 9 weeks. Client industry: gaming._
