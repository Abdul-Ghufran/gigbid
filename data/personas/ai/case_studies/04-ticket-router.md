# Support-ticket triage with transformers

## The problem
The product team was drowning in manual work that should have been automated.

## What I did
- The solution relied on made sense of free-text tickets; I set up that part myself to make results reproducible.
- I delivered the piece that handles pretrained models from the Hub to hit the client's launch date.
- Used FastAPI to keep latency low under load.

## Outcome
The work routed 84% of tickets automatically with 93% accuracy.
