# Fine-tuned legal clause summariser

## The problem
Associates spent evenings summarising contract clauses for partners.

## What I did
- Used LLM fine-tuning to hit the client's launch date.
- I developed the core of it with Hugging Face, to keep latency low under load.
- I shipped the core of it with a deep-learning framework from Meta, to make results reproducible.

## Outcome
The work matched paralegal summaries on 88% of 200 audited clauses.

_Timeline: about 13 weeks. Client industry: legal._
