# Churn early-warning model for a telecom

## The problem
The carrier was losing prepaid subscribers and only noticed after they had left.

## What I did
- I built the core of it with scikit-learn, to reduce operating cost.
- Used gradient boosting to make results reproducible.
- The solution relied on dataframes; I implemented that part myself to hit the client's launch date.

## Outcome
The work flagged 71% of churners two weeks early at 0.62 precision.

_Timeline: about 11 weeks. Client industry: telecom._
