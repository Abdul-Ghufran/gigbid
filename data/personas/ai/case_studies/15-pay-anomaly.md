# Fraud anomaly detection for payments

## The problem
Risk decisions depended on brittle processes nobody fully trusted.

## What I did
- I set up the piece that handles XGBoost to keep latency low under load.
- I delivered the core of it with python3, to make results reproducible.
- I built the piece that handles amazon web services to hit the client's launch date.

## Outcome
The work caught 2.3x more fraud at the same review budget.
