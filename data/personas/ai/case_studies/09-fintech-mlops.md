# ML platform clean-up for a fintech

## The problem
Risk decisions depended on brittle processes nobody fully trusted.

## What I did
- I designed the piece that handles model monitoring to hit the client's launch date.
- The solution relied on MLflow; I developed that part myself to hit the client's launch date.
- I set up the piece that handles containers so the system scales cleanly.
- Used ec2 to keep latency low under load.

## Outcome
The work cut model release time from 2 weeks to 1 day.
