# Policy Q&A assistant for an insurance broker

## The problem
Brokers were digging through hundreds of PDF policies to answer simple customer questions.

## What I did
- Used RAG to reduce operating cost.
- I designed the piece that handles chained LLM calls to reduce operating cost.
- Used Pinecone so the team could stop doing it by hand.
- Used a typed Python web API so the system scales cleanly.

## Outcome
The work cut average policy-lookup time from 9 minutes to 40 seconds.

_Timeline: about 10 weeks. Client industry: insurance._
