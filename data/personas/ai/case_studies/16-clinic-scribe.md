# Speech-to-notes for clinicians

## The problem
Clinical staff lost hours every shift to scheduling and paperwork.

## What I did
- Brought in Hugging Face to keep latency low under load.
- Used made sense of free-text tickets to keep latency low under load.
- I designed the core of it with FastAPI, so non-technical staff could use it.

## Outcome
The work saved doctors about 25 minutes per shift.

_Timeline: about 5 weeks. Client industry: healthcare._
