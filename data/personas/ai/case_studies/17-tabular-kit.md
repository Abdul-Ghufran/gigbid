# Kaggle-style tabular pipeline template

## The problem
Every new tabular project started from a blank notebook.

## What I did
- The solution relied on scikit-learn; I developed that part myself to reduce operating cost.
- I developed the piece that handles dataframes to make results reproducible.
- I implemented the core of it with CI/CD, to keep latency low under load.

## Outcome
The work used by 140 GitHub stargazers.
