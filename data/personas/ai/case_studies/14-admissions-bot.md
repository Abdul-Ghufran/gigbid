# Chatbot for a university admissions office

## The problem
Admissions staff answered the same questions hundreds of times a week.

## What I did
- Used RAG so non-technical staff could use it.
- The solution relied on LangChain; I implemented that part myself so the team could stop doing it by hand.
- I developed the core of it with fastapi backend, so the team could stop doing it by hand.

## Outcome
The work answered 72% of applicant questions without staff.

_Timeline: about 12 weeks. Client industry: education._
