## Auth and transport details

- **marketplace**: Bearer: GigBid-signed per-user token (HMAC, 10 min) over HTTP; per-user token bucket 40 req/min; upstream credential = user's GigSim token or Upwork OAuth token, decrypted from the encrypted store.
- **portfolio**: Bearer (per-user signed token) over HTTP; none over stdio (local, single user).
- **workspace**: Bearer (per-user signed token) over HTTP.
